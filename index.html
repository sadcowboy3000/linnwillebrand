Field Day

Short film, 2 min. 2025

A collaboration between students from the Royal College of Music (KMH) and Beckmans College of Design. I received a score to interpret freely and turn into a music video. This project was the outcome of a four-week motion design course, during which I modeled and animated everything in Blender. The visual concept draws on themes of heartbreak, release, and new growth. Music by Erasmus Talbot.

Ordkonst

Editorial design, 2023-2024

Layout and graphic design on behalf of Ordkonst. Responsible for all typesetting, layout and graphic material provided for print and web. Ordkonst is Akademiska Föreningen committee of literature at Lund’s University. Established in 1994, Ordkonst is published four times a year and each issue revolves around a theme and contains contributions from both established and non-established writers and artists.
Ordkonst gratefully accept contributions of various kinds publishing all kinds of fiction texts, essays, reviews and images.

Available for purchase @ AF-bogren, Lund, Malmö konsthall, Malmö, Papercut, Stockholm, Rönnells antikvariat, Stockholm, Møllegadens boghandel, Köpenhamn.

She's Iconic!

Interactive video installation, 2025

She's iconic explores the definition and what it means to be an icon. Sacred themes meets queer and pop culture in an interactive audio visual installation where the spectator is invited to upload their own icon to be displayed on the triptych.


Projection on wooden sculpture. Created with touchdesigner. Music by me.

Supervised by: Anneé Olofsson

Fram

Graphic design, 2025

A project where I was tasked with creating an identity for a no longer existing Swedish company. This included a new logo, custom typeface and a work uniform.
The company I chose was a bike and motorcycle manufacturer named Fram. I took inspiration from the visual culture in competitive racing when I developed the identity.


The work wear was shown at a runway show in collaboration with Kvartalsrapport.


Head of Course: Laslo Strong
Video by: Radin Esmaeilzadeh
Hosted by: Third Culture Kids
